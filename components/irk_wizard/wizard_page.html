Clear any old pairing

If this phone has paired with this device before, it will silently reuse the old pairing and no new key is captured. This is the most common reason a capture never happens.

On your phone: open Bluetooth settings, find IRK Capture, and choose Forget This Device (iPhone: tap the ⓘ first). If it is not listed, nothing to do.
