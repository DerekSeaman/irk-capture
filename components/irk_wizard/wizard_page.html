Step 3 of 5

Clear any old pairing

If your device has paired with this ESP32 before, it may silently reuse the old pairing and no new key will be captured. This is the most common reason a capture never happens.

On your device open Bluetooth settings, find the prior advertised name and choose Forget This Device. Apple tip: tap the ⓘ first. If it is not listed, nothing to do.
